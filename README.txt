AVANS website
index.html is the complete site (logos, fonts and styles are built in). Open it in a browser or upload it to any host.
assets/ holds the favicon, shield and wordmark as transparent PNGs for reuse.

To finish before launch:
1. In index.html search for hello@avans.example and replace with your real email (one place in the script: var EMAIL).
2. Set var WA to your WhatsApp number in international format without + (e.g. 2547XXXXXXXX) so Ask/Request a quote open WhatsApp.
3. Fill the highlighted [your full name or registered business name] and [town] placeholders in the Terms and Privacy pages.
4. If you host the site yourself, update the Hosting paragraph in the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
5. Have a Kenyan lawyer review the legal pages and check ODPC registration.
